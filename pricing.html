Audit Pricing & Engagement FAQs

Can we upgrade our evaluation scope during the audit?
Yes, you can upgrade from Core Audit to Full WCAG AA at any time. Your initial assessment investment is applied directly to the expanded audit scope.
Do you offer specialized non-profit pricing?
Yes, we provide standard 20% rate adjustments for verified non-profit institutions and educational organizations.
What is the standard turnaround time for an audit report?
A standard WCAG 2.1 AA evaluation takes 10–15 business days depending on application complexity and dynamic state count.
Do audit packages include technical remediation support?
Yes, our Full WCAG AA and Enterprise packages provide direct code remediation snippets, DOM line locations, and developer Q&A support.
